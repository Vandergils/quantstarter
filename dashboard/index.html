Numbers

What do these mean?
Strategy vs buy and hold return
Did the idea beat simply holding the coin?
Max drawdown
The worst fall from a peak. Could you sit through it?
Sharpe ratio
Return per unit of risk. Above 1 is decent, below 0 means it lost money.
Trades
Every trade pays the fee. Very few trades means the result may be luck.
Win rate
Share of trades that made money. Low is fine if the wins are much bigger than the losses.
Training vs unseen test
If the idea only works on the training part, it probably fit past noise (overfitting).
